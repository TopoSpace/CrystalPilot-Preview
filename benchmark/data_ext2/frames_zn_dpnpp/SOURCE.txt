why selected:
  1) Fills the chemistry gap: all 8 existing data_frames sets are organic or
     molecular inorganic - this is a Zn coordination polymer.
  2) Legacy OD SAPPHIRE 3.0 format: tests graceful typed rejection at frame
     import (engine must not crash or silently mis-import).
  3) Twin stress with vendor ground truth: two-component CrysAlisPro twin
     reduction (HKLF4 x2 + HKLF5) shipped alongside the raw frames, and the
     published model is an inversion twin (BASF 0.496) - lets us test the
     hkl-entry stage on real twin data even while frames remain unimportable,
     and gives an authoritative target for any future format-rescue work.
