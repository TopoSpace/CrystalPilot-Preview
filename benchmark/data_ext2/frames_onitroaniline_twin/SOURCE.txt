compound: gamma-o-nitroaniline C6 H6 N2 O2 (metastable polymorph)
  space group P 21/a (non-standard setting, kept for literature compatibility);
  pseudo-orthorhombic B-centred superlattice
twinning (the point of this dataset): non-merohedral 2-component twin;
  twin operation = 180 deg rotation about c (equivalently: stacking fault with
  1/2 a translation exchanging the two independent molecules). Published ladder
  from the RDL, to benchmark any twin handling quantitatively:
    - naive single-domain integration + SHELXL:  R1 = 0.0678, wR2 = 0.2390
    - PLATON TWINROTMAT detwin -> HKLF5 SHELXL:  R1 = 0.0465, wR2 = 0.1332
    - EVAL15 simultaneous two-lattice integration: better still (RDL conclusion:
      "processing using twin lattices is by far superior to efforts to de-twin
      processed structure factors")

why selected:
  1) Second twinned raw-frames case next to data_frames/frames_bruker_apex2_laspartic
     (same lab + instrument, different compound and twin law): enables
     tune-on-one / verify-on-the-other experiments for frames-level twin rescue,
     the engine's known blind spot.
  2) The RDL publishes the exact R1/wR2 outcomes of wrong vs right twin handling,
     giving objective targets for capability-boundary scoring.
  3) Documented trap sequence reproducible from frames: wrong SG guess (P21/a
     absences violated by twin overlap), high merging residuals - tests honest
     diagnostics instead of silent bad refinement.
